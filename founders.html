Who this is for

You're building a company and you're the one everyone looks to. The roadmap is clear on paper. The harder parts are not: who to hire, how to lead them, when to say no, and how to keep your own head clear when everything lands on you.

You want someone who has been there, will be straight with you, and won't hand you a list of slogans.

What we work on

  • Leading people. How you show up for your team, and how to build a culture that holds when you're not in the room.
  • Big decisions. Getting clear before you hire, raise, pivot or let someone go.
  • Your own energy. Staying steady through the ups and downs, so the company doesn't run on your exhaustion.

We start with what's most pressing for you. No fixed script.

How it works

First

A conversation

We talk about where you are and what you want to change. We both decide if it's a fit.

Then

6 to 9 one-to-one sessions

Built around what you're facing right now. Between sessions you try things, and we look at what happened.

At the end

You leave with

A clearer way of making decisions, a plan for your team, and the habits to keep going without me.

We'll talk about the investment on the call.
